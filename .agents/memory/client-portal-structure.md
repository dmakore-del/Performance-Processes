---
name: Client portal structure
description: User-requested primary navigation and purpose of the client portal.
---

The client-facing portal has four main areas: Company Overview (company and engagement summary), Payments (invoices and receipts), Progress (requests, document exchange, and team chat), and Reports. Other client pages should be consolidated into these areas or removed from client navigation. Staff and admin work areas remain separate.

**Why:** the user asked to simplify the portal around these four client needs while keeping account operations functional.

**How to apply:** when adding client features, place them in one of these areas rather than creating another top-level client tab.
