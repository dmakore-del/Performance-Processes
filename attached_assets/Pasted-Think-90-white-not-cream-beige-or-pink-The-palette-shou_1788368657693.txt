Think 90% white, not cream, beige or pink.
The palette should be closer to:
White #FFFFFF — main background
Soft off-white #FAFAF8 — alternating sections
Near black #171717 — typography
Blush #E8C4CB — small brand accent
Very pale blush #F8EFF1 — occasional section background
Mid grey #717171 — secondary copy
The blush should appear in things such as a thin line, button hover, small label, number, icon background or one subtle section. It shouldn't become the site's main colour.
I would also avoid the image above that is predominantly pink. That goes too far into female entrepreneur/branding agency territory rather than professional consultancy.

What I would tell your developer
Create a light, sophisticated boutique business consultancy website. The site should be predominantly crisp white with black typography and extremely restrained blush-pink accents. It should feel professional, established, intelligent and premium while remaining warm and approachable. Use generous white space, strong typographic hierarchy, editorial business photography, fine borders and structured grid layouts. Avoid dark backgrounds, oversized colour blocks, gradients, rounded SaaS-style cards, excessive icons and overly feminine design elements. The visual reference should sit between a modern management consulting firm and a high-end professional-services brand.
