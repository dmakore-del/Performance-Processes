# Phase 1 (Weeks 1–4): Build a Lead Engine

Objective: **Create predictable conversations with potential clients.**

## 1. Define Your Ideal Client Profile

Be extremely specific.

Example structure:

**Industry**

* SaaS
* Tech startups
* Professional services firms
* Operations-heavy companies

**Company size**

* 10–200 employees

**Pain points**

* Inefficient internal workflows
* Scaling challenges

**Decision makers**

* Founder / CEO
* COO
* Head of Operations

This clarity improves outreach success dramatically.

# 2. Create a High-Value Entry Offer

Most consulting businesses struggle because they try to sell **large projects immediately**.

Instead create a **low-friction first engagement**.

Examples:

### Process Audit

$1,000 – $3,000

Deliverables:

* Workflow mapping
* Bottleneck analysis
* Automation opportunities
* 30–60 day improvement roadmap

Benefits:

* Easy yes for clients
* Leads to larger engagements

---

### Operational Efficiency Workshop

Half-day or full-day session.

Outcome:

* Identify operational inefficiencies
* Align leadership on improvements
* Define process improvement roadmap

---

### Fractional Process Consultant

Monthly retainer:
$3k–$10k / month

Services:

* Systems implementation
* Process design
* Operational optimization

# 3. Start Daily LinkedIn Outreach

This will be your **primary lead generation engine** early on.

Daily target:

* **20 new connection requests**
* **10 meaningful conversations**
* **3 calls booked per week**

Example connection message:

Hi [Name], I work with growing companies to streamline their internal processes and eliminate operational bottlenecks. I came across your profile and thought it would be great to connect.

After connection:

Thanks for connecting! Out of curiosity — are you currently doing any process improvement work as your company grows? I often see teams hit operational friction around the 20–50 employee stage.

Goal: **start conversations, not sell immediately.**
