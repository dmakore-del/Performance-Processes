# Overall Rating

**6 / 10**

The page has the **right idea (thought leadership)** but currently feels **underdeveloped and under-leveraged**.

An Insights page is usually one of the **most powerful pages for consulting firms**, because it demonstrates expertise and builds trust through knowledge.

Large consulting firms like EY and KPMG use insights hubs to publish research, articles, and analysis that help clients understand trends and make strategic decisions. ([KPMG][1])

Right now, the page doesn’t yet function as a **true thought-leadership hub**.

---

# What the Page Does Well 👍

## 1. Correct Strategic Idea

Having an **Insights section** is excellent.

This is where consulting firms show:

* expertise
* frameworks
* research
* industry perspectives

Top consulting firms rely heavily on this approach because clients often **read insights before hiring consultants**.

---

## 2. Aligns With Consulting Brand

The page fits your positioning around:

* performance improvement
* leadership alignment
* organizational effectiveness

Thought leadership content reinforces credibility in these areas.

---

# Biggest Problems

## 1. Not Enough Content

The biggest issue is **volume**.

An insights section should ideally contain:

* articles
* frameworks
* research
* guides
* case studies

Right now it likely feels **too sparse**.

High-performing consulting firms often publish **dozens or hundreds of insight articles** to demonstrate expertise and authority.

---

## 2. No Content Categories

Visitors should be able to browse topics easily.

Example categories:

* Leadership
* Organizational Performance
* Strategy Execution
* Performance Management
* Nonprofit Leadership

Without categories, the page becomes **harder to explore**.

---

## 3. Missing Visual Article Cards

Good insights pages use **blog-style cards** with:

* title
* short description
* image or icon
* date

Example layout:

```
Article Title
2–3 sentence summary
Read More →
```

Right now it likely appears **too minimal or text-only**.

---

## 4. No Clear Value Proposition

The page should clearly state **why the insights matter**.

Example:

> Insights on leadership, performance, and organizational effectiveness to help leaders improve execution and results.

Without that framing, visitors may not know what to expect.

---

## 5. No Lead Generation Strategy

Insights pages should be **lead generation tools**.

Common consulting tactics:

* downloadable whitepapers
* reports
* guides
* newsletter signup

Example:

```
Download our guide:
Improving Organizational Performance

[Download PDF]
```

---
