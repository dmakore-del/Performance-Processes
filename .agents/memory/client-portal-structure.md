---
name: Client portal structure
description: User-requested primary navigation and purpose of the client portal.
---

The portal has exactly four primary navigation areas for every role: Company Overview, Payments, Progress, and Reports. Team management tools belong within these workspaces as role-restricted actions, not extra primary navigation items.

**Why:** the user explicitly corrected the earlier decision to preserve a longer staff/admin menu; they want these four sections throughout the portal while keeping account operations functional.

**How to apply:** when adding client features, place them in one of these areas rather than creating another top-level client tab.

Clients need to see the services they selected or were assigned by the admin, service-relative delivery progress, and real business improvements resulting from those services. Team and client views must use the same records. Delivery completion and measured business outcomes are distinct; do not present one as proof of the other or prefill invented results.

**Why:** the user wants the portal aligned with the amended website services and business goals, not generic organizational reporting. They specifically cited website-service results and adding past paid invoices to a client's profile.

**How to apply:** use relevant service-specific reporting suggestions with actual team-entered measurements and baselines; recording an already-paid invoice must not start a new charge.
