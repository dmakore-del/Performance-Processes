Please add **Training Programs** to the website as a new offering category that supports our existing business model.

Important:
Training Programs should **not** be presented as a separate business line or as generic training services. They should be positioned as an extension of our Growth Infrastructure System and framed as practical implementation training that helps individuals and teams apply real systems inside their organizations.

Overall objective

The purpose of adding Training Programs is to create an additional access point for clients who want support but may not yet be ready for a larger consulting engagement. This offering should appeal especially to:

* small businesses
* nonprofits
* professionals
* teams that want implementation support in a more structured or scalable format

Training should sit between Playbooks and higher-touch services such as Guided Implementation and Full System Build.

Core positioning

Please position Training Programs as:
**System Implementation Training**

Suggested positioning language:
“We do not offer generic business training. We provide practical training programs that help leaders and teams implement real systems for growth, execution, and performance.”

Suggested supporting message:
“Our training programs help organizations strengthen structure, improve execution, and build the systems required for sustainable growth.”
