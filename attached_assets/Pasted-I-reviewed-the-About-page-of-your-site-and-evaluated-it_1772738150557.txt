# Biggest Design Issues

## 1. Missing a Strong Founder/Leadership Presence

Most consulting firms make the **About page about people**.

Right now it feels like **a company description**, not a story.

Visitors want to know:

* Who leads the firm
* Their experience
* Why they started it

### Improvement

Add a founder section:

Example structure:

```
Meet Our Founder

Photo
Name
Title

Short story:
• background
• consulting experience
• why Performance Processes exists
```

This dramatically increases **trust and relatability**.

---

## 2. Not Enough Visual Elements

The page is **text-heavy**.

Modern consulting sites usually include:

* team photos
* icons
* short highlight blocks
* quotes
* visual separators

### Fix

Add elements like:

* Founder photo
* Value icons
* “Our Philosophy” graphic
* A quote block

---

## 3. The Mission/Values Section Could Be Stronger

Mission statements often become **generic**.

Instead of just stating values, show **how they appear in practice**.

Example:

Instead of:

> We believe in improving performance.

Use:

```
Our Values

Practical Impact
We focus on measurable outcomes, not theoretical advice.

Leadership Alignment
We work directly with leadership teams to create clarity and accountability.

Sustainable Change
Our work ensures improvements continue long after the engagement ends.
```

---

## 4. Missing Trust Signals

A strong About page includes credibility markers like:

* years of experience
* industries served
* client results
* certifications

Even a small section helps.

Example:

```
Trusted by leaders across:

• Corporate organizations
• Nonprofit institutions
• Growth-stage companies
```

---

## 5. No Call-to-Action

Most About pages end with a **conversion step**.

Right now it likely ends without one.

Add:

```
Ready to strengthen your organization's performance?

[Book a Discovery Call]
```
