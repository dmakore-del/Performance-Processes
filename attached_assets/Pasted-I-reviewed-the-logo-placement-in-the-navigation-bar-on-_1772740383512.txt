# Areas for Improvement

## 1. Logo Size Could Be Slightly Larger

The logo currently appears **a bit small relative to the header height**.

This reduces brand visibility.

### Ideal guideline

The logo height should occupy about:

**60–70% of the navbar height**

Example:

If navbar height = 80px
Logo height ≈ **45–55px**

This makes the brand **more recognizable and balanced**.

---

## 2. Vertical Alignment

The logo appears **slightly high in the header area**.

It should be perfectly **center-aligned vertically**.

Small adjustments improve polish.

---

## 3. Left Padding

The logo could use **slightly more spacing from the browser edge**.

Current spacing feels a little tight.

Recommended:

**24–40px padding from the left edge**

This gives a more premium feel.

---

## 4. No CTA Button in Navbar

The navbar currently appears to only contain links.

Consulting websites often include a **primary CTA in the navbar**, such as:

**Book a Strategy Session**

Example layout:

```
Logo | Services | Approach | Insights | About | [Book a Strategy Session]
```

This increases **lead conversion**.
