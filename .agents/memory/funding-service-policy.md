name: Funding service authorization policy
description: Private grant/financing workflow and how to handle conflicting rates in the supplied agreement.
---

Grants and financing need a separate private, per-service authorization and payment flow, not a generic recurring retainer or public pricing page.

**Why:** the user supplied a separate grant/financing retainer and said these admin functions differ from other services. They previously directed that costs remain private and be shared after discovery.

**How to apply:** preserve client-approved scope, quantities, caps and timing before billing. Signup service interests do not authorize work. Admin impersonation must never substitute for the client's fee approval. Research billing is for suitable delivered results, hourly billing is for actual time at month-end, and percentage fees depend on qualifying funds actually received.

The supplied agreement contradicts itself on complex-application and business-plan rates. Require an explicit client-approved quotation instead of silently choosing one of those figures.

**Why:** treating either conflicting amount as definitive could misstate the agreed fee.

**How to apply:** resolve future fee changes through a confirmed quotation or amended agreement; do not infer authoritative terms from old uploaded pricing tables.
