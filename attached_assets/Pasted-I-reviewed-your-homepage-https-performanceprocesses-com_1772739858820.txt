# Biggest Homepage Issues

## 1. Weak Hero Section

The **hero (top section)** is the most important part of the homepage.

Right now it likely:

* explains the company
* but doesn’t strongly **communicate value or outcomes**

A strong consulting hero usually has:

**1 headline**
**1 value proposition**
**1 clear CTA**

Example improvement:

> **Improve Organizational Performance and Strategic Alignment**
> We help leadership teams strengthen strategy execution, accountability, and measurable results.

Button:

**Book a Strategy Session**

---

## 2. Not Outcome-Focused Enough

Consulting buyers care about **results**.

Your messaging leans more toward **concepts** rather than outcomes.

Example improvement:

Instead of:

> Organizational performance consulting

Use:

> Helping organizations improve leadership alignment, strategy execution, and measurable performance outcomes.

---

## 3. Lack of Visual Frameworks

Your site is **very text-oriented**.

High-end consulting sites show:

* frameworks
* process diagrams
* methodology graphics

Example:

**Performance Improvement Model**

```
Assess
→ Align
→ Implement
→ Measure
```

Frameworks dramatically increase **perceived expertise**.

---

## 4. Missing Trust Signals

The homepage should quickly show credibility.

Examples:

* industries served
* experience
* results
* testimonials
* clients

Even simple statements help.

Example:

> Supporting corporate, nonprofit, and mission-driven organizations.

---

## 5. Weak Call-To-Action Placement

The homepage should have **multiple CTAs**.

Example:

Top section
→ **Book a Strategy Session**

Mid page
→ **Discuss Your Organizational Challenges**

Bottom
→ **Schedule a Consultation**

---
