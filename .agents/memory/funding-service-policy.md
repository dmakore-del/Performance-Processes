---
name: Funding service authorization policy
description: Private grant/financing workflow and how to handle conflicting rates in the supplied agreement.
---

Grants and financing retain a separate private, per-service authorization workflow for client-specific work. Approved itemized starting prices may now be public with payment buttons.

**Why:** the user supplied a separate grant/financing retainer and said these admin functions differ from other services. They subsequently approved the modular pricing document for public prices and checkout, superseding the previous private-only pricing instruction.

**How to apply:** preserve client-approved scope, quantities, caps and timing before delivering work. Signup service interests do not authorize work. Admin impersonation must never substitute for the client's fee approval. New public funding services use fixed or milestone fees, not success fees or percentages. Do not silently rewrite existing signed agreements.

The supplied agreement contradicts itself on complex-application and business-plan rates. Require an explicit client-approved quotation instead of silently choosing one of those figures.

**Why:** treating either conflicting amount as definitive could misstate the agreed fee.

**How to apply:** resolve future fee changes through a confirmed quotation or amended agreement; do not infer authoritative terms from old uploaded pricing tables.
