# Developer Brief: Performance Processes Client Portal Rebuild

## 1. Project Overview

We are requesting a complete redesign and rebuild of the **Performance Processes Soleria Inc. Client Portal**.

**Website:** https://performanceprocesses.com  
**Proposed portal URL:** https://portal.performanceprocesses.com

The objective is to create a sophisticated, secure, fully responsive client management platform that serves as the central hub for all Performance Processes services.

Our business specializes in:

- Business incorporation and company setup
- Business consulting and advisory services
- Website design, development, and digital presence
- Grant research, preparation, and application management
- Business financing assistance
- Business plans, financial projections, and cash-flow forecasting
- Ongoing business support and monthly retainer services

The portal must streamline our client experience while reducing administrative work for our internal team.

**This is intended to be a complete functional rebuild, not merely a visual redesign.** Existing functionality, data, and integrations should be audited before development begins to determine what can be retained or migrated.

---

## 2. Branding and Visual Identity

### Logo and Brand Colours

Use the official Performance Processes logo provided as the primary visual reference.

The portal's colour palette must be extracted from and aligned with our existing logo.

Our logo features vibrant pink, magenta, blush pink, and deep burgundy tones alongside elegant charcoal typography.

Suggested starting palette (approximate values; confirm against the original logo asset):

| Colour | HEX | Application |
|---|---|---|
| Signature Pink | #F72B78 | Primary buttons, active navigation, interactive accents |
| Deep Magenta | #BD1359 | Hover states, progress indicators, selected elements |
| Burgundy | #80002E | Premium accents and secondary highlights |
| Soft Blush | #FFF0F5 | Subtle backgrounds, highlighted content |
| Charcoal | #252525 | Primary headings and body text |
| White | #FFFFFF | Main backgrounds and cards |
| Light Grey | #F7F7F8 | Dashboard background and section separation |

### Design Direction

The portal should feel like a premium business consulting and financial advisory platform.

The desired aesthetic is:

- Modern, sophisticated, and minimalist
- Elegant and professional without appearing overly corporate
- Clean white backgrounds and spacious layouts
- Strong typography with clear visual hierarchy
- Subtle rounded cards and refined borders
- Minimal visual clutter
- Strategically used pink and burgundy accents
- Polished icons, charts, and progress indicators
- Smooth, subtle transitions and microinteractions

**Important:** Avoid overwhelming the interface with pink. White and neutral tones should dominate, while our logo colours provide distinction.

Use the full logo on the login screen and appropriately sized branding in the dashboard navigation. Do not redesign or alter the company logo.

The interface must be responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices and meet WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility standards.

## 3. User Roles and Permissions

Create separate user experiences with role-based access control.

### Client Users

Clients should only access their own organization's information.

They must be able to:

- View active and completed services
- Track project progress
- Access documents and agreements
- Submit requests
- Communicate with assigned consultants
- Review and pay invoices
- Book appointments
- Track grant and financing applications
- Approve quotations, proposals, and deliverables
- Manage their business profile

Support multiple authorized users under one corporate client account, with permissions controlled by a designated client administrator.

### Internal Staff

Authorized Performance Processes employees and consultants should be able to:

- Manage assigned clients
- Create and update projects
- Assign tasks and milestones
- Upload deliverables
- Manage grant opportunities and applications
- Send communications
- Issue quotations and invoices
- Review client-submitted documents
- Track application deadlines
- Generate reports

### System Administrator

Administrators require access to organization-wide settings, client accounts, service configurations, staff permissions, reporting, billing administration, and audit logs.

Financial records and sensitive documents must remain subject to appropriate permission restrictions, including for internal staff.

## 7. Grant & Financing Centre

This is a priority feature and should be developed as a comprehensive standalone module.

### A. Grant Opportunity Management

Performance Processes administrators should be able to add relevant grant and financing opportunities to individual client accounts.

Each opportunity should display:

- Grant or financing program name
- Funding organization
- Potential funding amount
- Eligibility criteria
- Application deadline
- Eligible expenses
- Required documentation
- Performance Processes' preliminary assessment
- Recommended next steps

Clients should be able to indicate whether they are interested in proceeding.

### B. Application Management

Once a client authorizes an application, the system should create a dedicated application workspace.

Track stages such as:

- Eligibility assessment
- Application authorized
- Information collection
- Draft preparation
- Internal review
- Client approval
- Submission
- Under funder review
- Additional information requested
- Approved
- Declined
- Withdrawn

Each application must have its own documents, fees, deadlines, communications, and status history.

### C. Funding and Fee Tracking

Display:

- Amount requested
- Amount approved
- Amount received
- Application preparation fees
- Outstanding invoices
- Applicable success fees

For our current grant and financing arrangements, the portal must support a **6% contractual success fee**, where applicable under the signed agreement, plus applicable HST.

All rates and fee calculations must be configurable by authorized administrators and tied to the specific client agreement.

Do not automatically invoice success fees based solely on a change in application status. Billing must follow the agreed contractual trigger.

### D. Post-Award Management

Include the ability to track reporting requirements, funding disbursements, documentation requests, and funder deadlines.

The system must not imply that Performance Processes guarantees funding approval.

## 10. Document Management & E-Signatures

Create a secure centralized document library.

Features must include:

- File uploads and downloads
- Organized folders by client, project, and service
- Document categorization
- File version history
- Secure document previews
- Client and staff permissions
- Downloadable completed deliverables
- Document request checklists
- Electronic signature functionality or integration

Support common formats including PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PNG, and JPG.

Documents may include incorporation records, business plans, financial statements, agreements, invoices, grant applications, and funding correspondence.

Use a compliant e-signature provider and retain signature evidence and audit trails.

## 11. Billing, Invoices & Payments

Build a billing section where clients can manage their financial relationship with Performance Processes.

### Client Features

- View all invoices
- Pay outstanding invoices
- Download receipts
- View transaction history
- Access signed agreements
- Review recurring retainer charges
- View payment due dates
- Update authorized payment methods securely
- Review quotations and approve charges

### Administrator Features

- Create invoices
- Assign fees to projects
- Configure recurring retainers
- Track overdue payments
- Issue credits and adjustments
- Configure applicable taxes
- Manage service pricing
- Track success fees
- Export financial reports

Integrate with a reputable payment processor such as Stripe.

Credit card data should not be stored directly on our servers. Use payment-provider-hosted or tokenized payment methods.

The system should support CAD as the primary billing currency, with other currencies configurable if required.

## 12. Client Communication & Support

Provide a centralized messaging and support system.

Clients should be able to:

- Message their assigned consultant
- Submit service requests
- Ask questions about active projects
- Attach files to conversations
- Receive status updates
- View previous correspondence

Support tickets should include reference numbers, priority levels, assignment, and status tracking.

Internal notes must never be visible to clients.

Email notifications should inform clients of important portal updates without exposing sensitive attachments or confidential financial information in notification emails.

The system should accommodate integrations with Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook, or an appropriate scheduling platform.

## 17. Security and Privacy

Security is a fundamental requirement because the portal will process business registration information, financial records, contracts, and other confidential client data.

Implement:

- Secure authentication and password recovery
- Multifactor authentication
- Role-based access controls
- Strict separation of each client organization's data
- Encryption in transit and at rest
- Secure document storage
- Administrative audit logs
- Session management
- Rate limiting and intrusion protection
- Backups and recovery procedures
- Retention and deletion controls
- Secure integration authentication
- Protection against common OWASP web application vulnerabilities

The system must be designed with applicable Canadian privacy obligations, including PIPEDA where applicable, in mind.

Do not use sensitive client financial documents or confidential records for AI training.

---

## 18. Technical Requirements

The rebuilt portal must be:

- Fully responsive
- Fast and performant
- Secure and scalable
- Accessible
- Modular and maintainable
- Compatible with modern browsers
- Connected to the existing Performance Processes website
- Capable of supporting future integrations

The developer should recommend an appropriate modern technology stack rather than selecting one solely for visual convenience.

All displayed project statuses, invoices, messages, and documents must be backed by functioning data services.

**Do not deliver a static dashboard with placeholder features presented as operational functionality.**

Provide a clearly documented technical architecture, source code, administrative credentials through secure handover, deployment instructions, and integration documentation.

---

## 19. Existing Portal Migration

Before the rebuild, conduct an audit of the current portal.

Identify:

- Existing features and workflows
- Client accounts
- Documents and project records
- Payment and billing integrations
- Existing databases
- Authentication processes
- Features requiring replacement
- Data that needs to be migrated

Prepare a migration plan that minimizes interruption to existing clients.

Do not delete or overwrite existing production data without approval and a verified recovery plan.

## 22. Overall Vision

We want Performance Processes to deliver a client experience comparable to a sophisticated professional services firm.

The portal should communicate **professionalism, transparency, efficiency, and trust**.

Clients should be able to log in at any time and understand:

- What services they are receiving
- What Performance Processes is currently working on
- What progress has been made
- What documents or decisions are required from them
- What payments are outstanding
- What happens next

The end result should be an elegant, highly functional digital environment that makes working with Performance Processes simple, organized, and professional.

**The visual direction must remain consistent with our official pink, magenta, burgundy, white, and charcoal brand identity throughout the entire experience.**

Please review this specification and provide the proposed architecture, implementation approach, development timeline, and any recommendations for improving the functionality and user experience before commencing development.
