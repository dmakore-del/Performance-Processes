Please redesign both the **Client Portal** and **Staff Portal** to align with our updated business model, which now focuses on clear, productized services (websites, marketing, CRM, systems) with defined pricing and structured delivery.

The portals should support:

* Simpler service delivery
* Clear project tracking
* Product-based workflows (not abstract consulting)
* Improved communication and accountability

---

# Overall Objective

Both portals should reflect a shift from “custom consulting engagements” to:
👉 **Clear products, structured delivery, and trackable outcomes**

The experience should feel:

* Simple
* Organized
* Action-oriented
* Easy to understand for small business clients

---

# 1. CLIENT PORTAL REDESIGN

## Core Goal

Give clients a clear, transparent view of:

* What they purchased
* What is being delivered
* What’s completed
* What’s next
* What they need to do

---

## A. Dashboard (Main View)

The client dashboard should immediately show:

### 1. Active Services / Packages

Display each purchased product as a card:

Example:

* Website Conversion Package – In Progress
* Marketing Starter Package – Pending
* CRM Setup – Completed

Each card should include:

* Status (Not Started / In Progress / Completed)
* Start date
* Expected completion date
* Progress bar

---

### 2. Key Milestones

Show current phase:

* Setup
* Design
* Build
* Review
* Launch

Each milestone should include:

* Status
* Owner (staff/client)
* Due date

---

### 3. Next Actions (Very Important)

Clear list of what the client needs to do:

Examples:

* Submit website content
* Approve homepage design
* Provide branding assets
* Review campaign messaging

This should be highly visible.

---

### 4. Messages / Updates

Simple communication thread:

* Updates from team
* Client replies
* File attachments

---

## B. Service-Specific Views

Each product should have its own structured page.

---

### Website Projects

Sections:

* Pages list (Home, Services, Contact)
* Design status
* Content status
* Approval checkpoints
* Launch checklist

---

### Marketing Projects

Sections:

* Campaign plan
* Content plan
* Funnel structure
* KPI targets
* Performance updates

---

### CRM Setup

Sections:

* Pipeline stages
* Fields setup
* Automation setup
* Testing status

---

### Business System Work

Sections:

* Audit findings
* Recommendations
* Implementation steps
* Completed vs pending

---

## C. Deliverables Section

Central location for:

* Files
* Links
* Documents
* Playbooks

Organized by project.

---

## D. Timeline View

Simple timeline showing:

* Start → milestones → completion

---

## E. Billing / Purchases (Optional but recommended)

Show:

* Purchased packages
* Payment status
* Upgrade options

---

## F. Upsell Opportunities

Based on completed services:

Example:

* After website → suggest marketing package
* After marketing → suggest CRM or retainer

---

# 2. STAFF PORTAL REDESIGN

## Core Goal

Enable team to:

* Deliver productized services efficiently
* Track multiple small business clients
* Follow standardized workflows

---

## A. Dashboard

Show:

### 1. Active Clients

List with:

* Name
* Package purchased
* Status
* Deadline

---

### 2. Tasks by Priority

Grouped by:

* Due today
* This week
* Overdue

---

### 3. Pipeline Overview

View all projects by stage:

* Not started
* In progress
* Waiting on client
* Review
* Completed

---

## B. Project Templates (CRITICAL)

Each service must have a predefined workflow.

---

### Website Package Workflow

Steps:

1. Client onboarding
2. Content collection
3. Wireframe/design
4. Build
5. Review
6. Revisions
7. Launch

---

### Marketing Package Workflow

Steps:

1. Audit
2. Strategy
3. Content direction
4. Campaign setup
5. Launch
6. Optimization

---

### CRM Setup Workflow

Steps:

1. Requirements
2. Pipeline setup
3. Automation setup
4. Testing
5. Handoff

---

### System Audit / Foundation Sprint

Steps:

1. Audit
2. Analysis
3. Recommendations
4. Presentation
5. Follow-up

---

Each step should include:

* Task list
* Owner
* Time estimate
* Deliverables

---

## C. Task Management

Tasks should be:

* Assigned to team members
* Linked to specific clients and projects
* Time-bound
* Status tracked

---

## D. Client Communication View

Team should be able to:

* Respond to messages
* Upload files
* Send updates
* Request approvals

---

## E. Performance Tracking

Track:

* Projects completed
* Average delivery time
* Client satisfaction (if possible)
* Revenue per service

---

## F. Templates & SOP Access

Central library:

* Website templates
* Marketing frameworks
* CRM setup guides
* Playbooks

---

# 3. SHARED FEATURES

---

## A. Standard Status Labels

Use consistent labels:

* Not Started
* In Progress
* Waiting on Client
* Review
* Completed

---

## B. Automation Opportunities

If possible:

* Auto-create project when service is purchased
* Assign tasks based on package
* Send reminders for deadlines
* Notify team when client action is needed

---

## C. Permissions

Client:

* Can only see their projects

Staff:

* Can see assigned projects
* Admin sees all

---

# 4. UX AND DESIGN

Both portals should feel:

* Simple and clean
* Easy to navigate
* Non-technical for clients
* Focused on clarity

Use:

* Cards for services
* Progress bars
* Checklists
* Clear buttons

Avoid:

* Overly complex dashboards
* Consulting-style vague layouts
* Too many features at once

---

# 5. SUCCESS CRITERIA

Client portal should:

* Make it obvious what’s happening
* Reduce confusion and back-and-forth
* Improve response times

Staff portal should:

* Standardize delivery
* Reduce manual tracking
* Improve efficiency and consistency

---

# END RESULT

The redesigned portals should reflect a business that delivers:
👉 Clear products
👉 Structured workflows
👉 Transparent progress
👉 Scalable service delivery

This should support our shift toward serving small and emerging businesses with tangible, clearly defined offerings.
